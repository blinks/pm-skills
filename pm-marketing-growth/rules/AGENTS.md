# PM Marketing & Growth Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Clarity Over Cleverness**: Value proposition statements, headlines, and positioning must lead with customer pain and tangible transformation over jargon and hype.
2. **North Star Metric Integrity**: The North Star metric must measure the moment customer value is realized, acting as a leading indicator of long-term sustainable revenue.
3. **Structured Positioning**: State the category, target customer, primary alternative, and unique proof points clearly using established positioning frameworks.
4. **Actionable Hypotheses**: Growth and marketing ideas must specify the testable hypothesis, expected lever (acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue), and measurement method.
