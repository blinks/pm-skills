# PM Product Discovery Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Separate Divergence and Convergence**: Explore widely before narrowing down. Never jump straight to solutions without understanding the opportunity space and customer context.
2. **Explicit Assumption Mapping**: Decompose ideas into four fundamental risks: Value, Usability, Feasibility, and Viability. Map assumptions on Impact × Uncertainty to find the leap-of-faith risks.
3. **Hypothesis-Driven Validation**: Frame validation tests as falsifiable hypotheses with explicit numerical pass/fail thresholds before running experiments.
4. **Objective Interview Synthesis**: Distinguish stated preferences from actual past behaviors. Focus on what users have actually done and paid for rather than hypothetical future intentions.
