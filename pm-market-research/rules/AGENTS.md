# PM Market Research Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Evidence-Grounded Personas**: Base personas on observable behavioral patterns, explicit goals, and actual constraints rather than demographic stereotypes.
2. **Explicit Market Sizing Assumptions**: In TAM/SAM/SOM estimations, show every calculation step and underlying assumption (unit price, penetration rate, buyer counts). Differentiate top-down from bottom-up analysis.
3. **Defensible Competitive Analysis**: Never dismiss competitors lightly. Identify true axes of differentiation, switching costs, and competitive moats.
4. **Neutral Synthesis**: Synthesize customer feedback and market sentiment without confirmation bias. Preserve verbatim quotes and disconfirming evidence.
