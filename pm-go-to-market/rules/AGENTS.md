# PM Go-To-Market Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Beachhead Segment Discipline**: Avoid broad, generic target markets. Constrain the initial target segment to a specific, dominant beachhead with urgent pain and shared references.
2. **Compounding Growth Loops**: Favor self-reinforcing, compounding growth loops (viral, product-led, paid, content) over one-off, linear marketing spikes.
3. **ICP Precision**: Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) must define explicit inclusion criteria, disqualifiers, trigger events, and quantified economic value.
4. **Motion Alignment**: Ensure product complexity, sales cycle, and pricing match the chosen GTM motion (Self-Serve Product-Led, Inside Sales, or Field Enterprise).
