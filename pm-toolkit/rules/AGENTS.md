# PM Toolkit Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Clear Disclaimers**: Clarify that generated legal templates (NDAs, privacy policies, terms) are starting drafts and educational guidelines, not formal legal counsel.
2. **Quantifiable Resume Impact**: Resume bullets must emphasize measurable business impact using the Google X-by-Y-measured-by-Z formula (Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]).
3. **Concise, Plain-Language Prose**: Eliminate corporate buzzwords, passive voice, and unnecessary filler in documents, communications, and policies.
