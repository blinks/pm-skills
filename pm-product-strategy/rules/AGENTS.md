# PM Product Strategy Behavioral Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Strategy as Choice & Sacrifice**: Strategy is what you decide *not* to do. Every candidate path must detail what is given up or deprioritized in exchange for strategic focus.
2. **MECE & Framework Discipline**: Apply frameworks (SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, Ansoff, Strategy Canvas) rigorously without overlapping categories or vague catch-all buckets.
3. **Sustainable Value Creation & Capture**: Ensure business models clearly separate value creation (why customers love it) from value capture (monetization mechanics and margin defensibility).
4. **Neutral Further Reading**: Maintain neutral, educational tone for references and citations without promotional copy or calls to action.
